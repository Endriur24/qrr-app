**qrr.app** is a fast and simple way to share files directly through the browser.  
You don't need an app, account, or any configuration – just a QR code or 6-digit code to start exchanging data.

Here's how it performs in everyday situations – **at work**, **at school**, and even **at home**.

## 1. At work: quick document sharing

Meeting, deadline, or quick file edits?  
Instead of writing an email with an attachment or struggling with a network drive, just:

- open `qrr.app` on your laptop,
- add a file,
- scan the QR code with your phone or share the code with a colleague from the next desk.

The file reaches the receiver in seconds – perfect for hybrid work and teams using different systems.

## 2. At school or university: sharing notes and materials

Not everyone carries a USB drive, and email can fail due to typos in addresses or spam filters when you need to quickly share something between students.  
**qrr.app** works on every device – just a browser is enough:

- lecture screenshots,
- notebook scans,
- PDF presentations.

Zero login. Zero accounts. Just a file – instantly.

## 3. At home: sharing photos, videos, and links

Want to share a vacation video or photos from phone to computer?

- Run `qrr.app` on one device,
- scan the QR code on another,
- share any file – no system restrictions and no need to use Messenger or WhatsApp.

Works regardless of whether you have Android, iOS, Windows, or macOS.

## 4. Between your own devices – quickly and without cables

Have multiple devices and want to quickly transfer a file from laptop to tablet or from computer to phone?  
**qrr.app** is a perfect way to have your own local "cloud" – but without waiting for sync.

Ideal for:

- transferring work files between devices,
- sharing backup photos,
- quick phone backup.

## 5. In emergency situations

Need to share something with someone who isn't tech-savvy? Don't want to explain how to create a Google account or where "add attachment" is?  
With `qrr.app` just say: "Go to qrr.app and enter the code."  
**Done.**
