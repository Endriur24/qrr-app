## Why do emails with attachments end up in spam?

Anti-spam filters have become extremely sensitive – and for good reason. Most spam today comes with suspicious links and files. But ordinary users suffer as a result.

Most common reasons why messages end up in spam:

- 📎 **Attachment in an unusual format** – `.zip`, `.exe`, `.js` can be immediately flagged as suspicious  
- 📧 **Message sent from an unauthorized address** – e.g., from a private server or company mailbox without SPF/DKIM  
- 🚫 **No content in the message** – just a file or a brief "here you go" looks suspicious to algorithms  
- 🔗 **Too many links or unusual domains** – increases the risk of being marked as spam  
- 🧠 **Too high sending frequency** – if you send many similar messages in a short time
