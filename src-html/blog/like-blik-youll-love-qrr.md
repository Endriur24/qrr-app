## How does qrr.app work?

Instead of typing a long email, searching for a USB drive, or struggling with cloud login:

1. Go to **[qrr.app](https://qrr.app)**  
2. Drop a file, paste a link, or type a message  
3. Show the **QR code** or **six-digit code**  
4. The receiver visits the site and instantly receives the data

No unnecessary steps.  
The connection is temporary and lasts only as long as needed.  
No accounts, no cloud, no history, no permanent records.
