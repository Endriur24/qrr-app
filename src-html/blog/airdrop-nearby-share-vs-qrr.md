**qrr.app** is an open alternative to AirDrop and Nearby Share – works in every browser, without installing apps or creating accounts.

## Why not AirDrop?

AirDrop only works on Apple devices. If you have a MacBook and iPhone – great. But if you want to share something with someone on Android, Windows, or Linux – the magic ends.

## Why not Nearby Share?

Similarly – Nearby Share is great, but only within the Google ecosystem.  
What if you have an iPhone or want to send a file to a Linux computer?

> "Just open **qrr.app** in a browser – on any device – and you're done. Connection lasts as long as the browser session. Zero traces."

## Why does qrr.app work everywhere?

- ✅ Browser = only requirement  
- ✅ Works on iOS, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux  
- ✅ No login or installation required  
- ✅ Can share session via code or QR code  

## How does it work?

The sender opens `https://qrr.app` and creates a session.  
They share the code or link with the receiver, who joins via browser.  
File sharing lasts as long as the session – after it ends, everything disappears.

## Summary

**qrr.app** is a lightweight and private form of instant transfer – perfect when you want to share something without traces, without apps, and without compatibility issues.
